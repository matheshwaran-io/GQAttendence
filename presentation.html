⚠️ THE PROBLEM LANDSCAPE

The Vulnerability of Modern University Classrooms

Static QR codes, manual paper call-outs, and biometric hardware suffer from four systemic vulnerabilities.

1. WhatsApp & Telegram QR Forwarding

A single present student snaps the static projector QR and texts it across campus. Absent students in hostels scan and punch in within seconds.

2. Buddy Punching & Multi-Account Swapping

One student logs out and sequentially logs into 5 friends' accounts on one physical phone, marking attendance for the whole group.

3. Mock GPS & Location Spoofing Apps

Android developer mock location apps easily deceive primitive campus geofences with hardcoded coordinates.

4. The 20% Lost Instruction Tax

Manual roll calls consume up to 15 minutes of every 45-minute lecture (33% instruction lost). Biometrics create long hallway queues.

Result: Inaccurate academic records and massive wasted teaching time.
